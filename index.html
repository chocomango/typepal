A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Make a little magic.

For a story stroll

Choose a story and type the passage exactly as it appears. Every word carries Puff a little farther. Spaces, capitals, and punctuation count; turn punctuation off whenever you like. Use Enter for a new paragraph and Backspace to correct a mistake.

Find your own pace. Typing stats and focus mode are optional, and there is no time limit. Each story remembers your place. Finish a journey to keep its postcard and earn a travel look for Puff.

For Typefall

Cute creatures are approaching Puff! Type the word above a creature to gently send it home. The first letter selects the closest matching friend. Keep typing to distinguish words with the same first letters. Starting a different word switches your target; Backspace releases it.

  1. Keep your three hearts. A creature reaching Puff costs a heart. No one gets hurt — Puff just needs a little rest.
  2. Grow with every word. Earn friendship XP and unlock cute titles and looks.
  3. Keep typing. Each wave brings faster friends and more words. Colored words become spells you can save for the right moment.

Type a colored word to collect it. Click its spell card or press its number to cast. Collecting a word keeps the spell safely in your pocket.

1 · breeze — slow for 7 seconds
2 · frost — freeze for 4 seconds
3 · aurora — send everyone home
4 · tide — give yourself some space
5 · bloom — restore a heart

Your titles and looks stay saved between adventures. Esc pauses either mode. Your game also takes a breather while this guide is open.
