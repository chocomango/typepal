A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Make a little magic.

Cute creatures are approaching Puff! Type the word above a creature to gently send it home. The first letter selects the closest matching friend. Keep typing to distinguish words with the same first letters. Starting a different word switches your target; Backspace releases it.

  1. Keep your three hearts. A creature reaching Puff costs a heart. No one gets hurt — Puff just needs a little rest.
  2. Grow with every word. Earn friendship XP and unlock cute titles and looks. Puff’s growth is cosmetic; you control the magic.
  3. Keep typing. Each wave brings faster friends and more words. Colored words become spells you can save for the right moment.

Type a colored word to collect it in your spell pocket. Click its card or press its number to cast. Collecting a word doesn’t activate its spell.

1 · breeze — slow for 7 seconds
2 · frost — freeze for 4 seconds
3 · aurora — clear all monsters
4 · tide — push monsters back
5 · bloom — restore a heart

Spells stack in your pocket for this adventure. Puff’s titles and looks stay saved between adventures.

Esc pauses · Backspace releases your target.
Mistakes don’t erase your progress. No WPM. No accuracy scores.
